What was wrong

The original terms were tagged only as 130 plain paragraphs with no headings, so a screen reader could not move between the 37 numbered sections; the file had no language set, some content was neither tagged nor marked as decoration, and its fonts were not embedded. veraPDF reported 1,245 failed checks under 13 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the document name as the main heading and all 37 numbered sections, from Definitions to Ownership of Work Product, as headings, reads the two columns in order, describes the logo, marks the page footers as decoration, and embeds every font. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
