We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Mobile Food Vehicle Fire Inspection and Safety Information (4 pages).

What was wrong

The original packet was not tagged at all, so a screen reader had no headings, no lists and no reading order to follow. It had no title, no language and no metadata stream, and its one link had no description. veraPDF reported 2,345 failed checks under 9 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the sheet's title as the main heading, its nine sections and their subsections as headings, every bulleted point and sub-point as a nested list, the Boulder Fire-Rescue badge described, the language set to English and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
