We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Student Acceptable Use Agreement, Form 2022F-1 (2 pages).

What was wrong

The original agreement was tagged, but it had no document title, a table in its tags had rows of unequal length, a font was missing information veraPDF requires, and the file did not identify itself as PDF/UA. veraPDF reported 4 failed checks under 4 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the agreement title as the main heading, its four sections, from Introduction to Parent/Guardian Opt Out, as headings, the sixteen kinds of unacceptable network use, A to P, as one list across both pages, the opt-out choices as a list, and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
