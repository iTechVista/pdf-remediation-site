We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Board of Trustees Meeting Minutes, July 20, 2023 (3 pages).

What was wrong

The original minutes had no tags at all, so a screen reader could not tell the agenda sections, paragraphs and lists apart, and the logo had no description. veraPDF reported 1,942 failed checks under 7 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the meeting's eleven sections, from Call to Order to Adjournment, as headings, the consent agenda items and budget points as lists, the logo described, and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
