Free sample for the City of Bryan, Texas

We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Board, Commission and Committee Application 2027 (2 pages).

What was wrong

The original application had no tags at all, so a screen reader could not tell the title, the list of boards and the return instructions apart, the City logo and the council district map had no descriptions, no language was set, and its only title was the Word file name. veraPDF reported 2,105 failed checks under 8 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the application title as the main heading, the sixteen boards, commissions and committees as a list, the return instructions under a heading for each way to return the form, the logo described, the district map page with its own heading, a description of the map, and the mayor and council members read in order, plus the language and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
