Free sample for the Ann Arbor District Library

We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Regular Board Meeting Minutes, April 29, 2024 (11 pages).

What was wrong

The original minutes had no tags at all, so a screen reader could not tell the agenda sections, motions and votes apart, and the heading repeated at the top of every page was read as part of the text. veraPDF reported 5,824 failed checks under 6 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the board's name as its heading, the agenda sections and their items as headings and subheadings, the repeated page header marked as decoration, and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
