We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Freedom of Information Request form (2 pages).

What was wrong

The original request form was tagged, but none of its 43 fill-in fields were part of the tag structure, so assistive technology could not tie a field to its label. veraPDF reported 53 failed checks under 9 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

In the remediated file all 43 fields have labels that are read aloud and sit in a sensible tab order, and the form has a title heading. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
