Free sample for Clark County, Washington

We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: ADA Facilities Survey: Center for Community Health, 3rd and 4th Floors (1 page).

What was wrong

The original survey had no tags at all, so a screen reader could not tell the title, findings, recommendations and photos apart, none of the five images had a description, no language was set, and some fonts were not embedded. veraPDF reported 173 failed checks under 9 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the survey title as the main heading, the two findings (the 3rd floor lobby drinking fountain and the break room pipe covering) as headings, the survey team and the recommendations as lists, the three photos and the ANSI protruding-objects diagram described by what they show, the county seal described, all fonts embedded, English set as the language, and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
