We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: Catering Menu (1 page).

What was wrong

The original menu was tagged as 120 separate paragraphs and seven layout tables with no headings, so a screen reader could not find the menu sections, two images had no description, the two links were not tagged, and the file had no title. veraPDF reported 14 failed checks under 8 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the menu title and its five sections, from Beverages to Salads, as headings with the platter and sandwich groups under them, the three platter size grids as small tables with the sizes and prices as column headers, each item read with its price, the Arapahoe Libraries logo described, the section icons marked as decoration, both links tagged, and a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
