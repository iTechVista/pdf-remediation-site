What was wrong

The original notice was a scanned image with no text at all, so a screen reader had nothing to read and the text could not be searched. veraPDF reported 9 failed checks under 5 PDF/UA-1 rules.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the scanned text recognised and checked word for word against the page, the notice title as a heading, the notice read as 4 paragraphs, and the scanned images behind the text marked as decoration. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
