We remediated one of your published documents, at no cost: CARES Act 2020 Institutional Disclosure (2 pages).

What was wrong

The original spreadsheet export had only a heading and two paragraph tags, so the funding table's award numbers, expenditures and balances were not tagged as a table with headers and could not be read cell by cell, and the file did not declare PDF/UA. veraPDF reported 1 failed check under 1 PDF/UA-1 rule.

What we fixed

The remediated file has the CARES Act Funding heading, a funds table with column headers (CARES Act Section, CFDA Number, DMACC Award Number, Award Amount) and a row header for each fund, and an expenditure table for each fund with every item as a row header next to its amount, plus a document title. It passes PDF/UA-1. The page looks the same as your original.
